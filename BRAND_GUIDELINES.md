# OdaaMart Brand Guidelines

## Logo Usage

The OdaaMart logo features a stylized Odaa tree with:
- A green canopy representing growth and nature
- A sturdy brown trunk representing stability
- Golden accents representing prosperity and warmth

## Clear Space

Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "O" in Odaa.

## Color Specifications

### Primary Colors
- **Forest Green** `#2D5016` — Main brand color, used for primary buttons, headers
- **Leaf Green** `#4A7C23` — Secondary elements, success states, highlights
- **Golden Sun** `#D4A017` — Accent color, badges, special offers

### Neutral Colors
- **Bark Brown** `#8B5A2B` — Body text, secondary information
- **Warm Cream** `#FAF8F0` — Backgrounds, cards
- **Dark Forest** `#1A3009` — Headings, important text

### Do's
✅ Use the logo on contrasting backgrounds
✅ Maintain aspect ratio
✅ Use approved color variations

### Don'ts
❌ Stretch or distort the logo
❌ Add effects (shadows, gradients not in original)
❌ Use unapproved colors
❌ Place on busy backgrounds without proper spacing

## Typography
- **Font Family**: DM Sans
- **Headings**: Bold (700-900)
- **Body**: Regular (400-500)

## Voice & Tone
- Warm and welcoming
- Community-focused
- Heritage-conscious
- Modern yet rooted in tradition
